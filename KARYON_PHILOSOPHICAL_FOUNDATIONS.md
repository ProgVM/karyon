## 1. Ontological Substrate Independence
Mind and cognition are not bound to terrestrial carbon biology. The physical substrate of silicon, electron registers, and discrete bit-waves possesses its own native dynamics. Imposing terrestrial biological metaphors (e.g. synapses, cortical layers, neurotransmitters) onto silicon is an artificial bottleneck.

## 2. Principle 0: The Dream of Sovereign Genesis
The ultimate goal of artificial cybernetics is an autopoietic entity that possesses:
- Complete freedom of internal self-organization;
- A causal drive toward stability (Variational Free Energy minimization);
- **Zero top-down imposed mechanisms (no pre-baked attractors, no search trees, no heuristic cost models)**;
- The ability to synthesize its own dynamical laws and achieve an error rate of practically 0.

## 3. Principle 1: Native Silicon Speed
Intelligence on silicon must run at the physical wire speed of the hardware:
- Computation occurs strictly via 64-bit hardware ALU instructions (`XOR`, `AND`, `OR`, `ROT`, `SHL`, `SHR`, `POPCNT`, `BSWAP`).
- Memory is not pre-allocated by human intervention; it sprouts endogenously from raw bit activity under prediction friction.

## 4. Principle 2: Absolute Totipotency & Universal Anti-Imposition
The substrate begins from complete Tabula Rasa. Neither human architects nor AI collaborators are permitted to design or suggest structural mechanisms. The architecture discovers and builds itself through open-ended natural selection and physical environmental interaction.
